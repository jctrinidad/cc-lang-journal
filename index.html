How the kana conversion works

Type standard romaji: sh→し, ch→ち, ts→つ, fu→ふ, and combos like kya→きゃ. Double a consonant for small っ (kk→っk…). Type n before a consonant, or nn, for ん. Use - for a long vowel mark (ー), and x or l before a vowel/tsu/ya·yu·yo for small kana (xtsu→っ). Keep Shift held down for every letter of a word (so it comes out ALL CAPS) to get katakana instead of hiragana — capitalizing just the first letter will only shift that one syllable.
